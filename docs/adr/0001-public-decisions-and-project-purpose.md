# ADR-0001: Public Decisions and Project Purpose

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-17

## Context

The project may be public. Anyone will be able to review its decisions, understand their rationale and limitations, and critique or reuse them as a reference. The goal is to develop this portfolio as a reference for future projects as well.

A project with this purpose may include more components and technologies than strictly necessary to deliver the portfolio itself.

## Decision

I accept that the project's decisions will be public and acknowledge the consequences of that visibility. I will use this project as a reference for future work and document the rationale and trade-offs behind relevant choices.

I also accept that the project may include more components and technologies than the minimum required when they support its purpose as a reference. The documentation should explain why those choices were made.

## Consequences

- The decisions and their trade-offs will be available for anyone to review, critique, and reuse.
- The broader technical scope may make the project more complex and increase its maintenance work.
- Components and technologies used as references are not automatically needed in future projects; each should be evaluated against that project's goals.
